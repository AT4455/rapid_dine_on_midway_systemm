About me

TG

I like turning messy datasets into clear insight — and ideas into working software.

I'm Thanuj Guptha Alavala, a Computer Science (AI & ML) undergraduate at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Kattankulathur, expected to graduate in 2027. I'm based in Chennai and happiest when I'm learning something new with a dataset in front of me.

My work sits between data and product: I build end-to-end pipelines, clean and prepare real-world data, train and evaluate machine-learning models, and ship the result as a full-stack application. Along the way I've worked with Python, SQL, MySQL, TensorFlow, Power BI, and Tableau — and I care about clear communication as much as clean code.

Outside the lab, I was Committee Head for Aaruush, SRM's national technical fest, coordinating logistics, venue setup, and cross-team execution. I'm currently looking for AI/ML and data-focused internships where I can learn from people who have shipped at scale.
